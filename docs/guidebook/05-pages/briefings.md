# Briefings and blog

**Purpose:** Short, accurate written reads on the data, with AI drafts approved by a person, plus occasional human-written posts.

## Must include
- [ ] Daily note (weekday evenings): today's releases, market close table, one chart
- [ ] Release notes for CPI, GDP, IIP, PLFS, MPC decision, trade
- [ ] Weekly briefing (Saturday)
- [ ] Monthly briefing
- [ ] Human-written posts ("From the editor")
- [ ] Each item shows "Drafted by AI, reviewed by <role>" or "Written by <author>"
- [ ] RSS per product
- [ ] Archive page with search and filter by product
- [ ] Corrections note at the bottom of any corrected item

## Must NOT include
- Any number not resolved from the ledger (see `07-editorial-policy.md`)
- Unattributed commentary, market rumours, or paywalled analysis
- Anything published without a human merge
