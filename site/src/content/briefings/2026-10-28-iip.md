**Industrial production grew 6.7% on a year earlier in September 2026**, compared with 7.8% in August 2026, according to MoSPI's quick estimate on the current base year.

Manufacturing output changed by 7.6%, mining by −6.2% and electricity by 11.2% (our calculations from the indices). Among use-based groups, capital goods changed by 10.4% and consumer durables by 6.0%.

The index of core industries, published earlier, changed by 7.9% in September 2026.
