**Real GDP grew 7.8% on a year earlier in Q1 FY27**, compared with 8.6% in Q4 FY26, according to MoSPI. Real gross value added grew 8.2% and nominal GDP 10.3%.

On the spending side, private consumption grew 7.1% and fixed investment 11.9% (our calculations from MoSPI's levels). Manufacturing value added grew 9.2% and agriculture 3.6%.
