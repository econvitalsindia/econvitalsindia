**Headline consumer price inflation was 5.54% in September 2026**, up 0.71 percentage points from 4.83% in August 2026, according to MoSPI. A year earlier it was 1.44%. RBI's target is 4%, with a tolerance band of 2–6%.

Core inflation, which excludes food and fuel (RBI's definition; our calculation from MoSPI's indices), was 4.33%, up 0.18 percentage points from the previous month. Inflation in food and beverages was 7.36% (up 1.68 percentage points) and in fuel 5.29% (up 0.08 percentage points).

By area, rural inflation was 6.08% and urban inflation 4.87%.
