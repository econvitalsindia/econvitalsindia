**The unemployment rate was 5.0% in September 2026**, unchanged from August 2026, according to MoSPI's monthly Periodic Labour Force Survey (people aged fifteen and over, current weekly status). A year earlier it was 5.2%.

Rural unemployment was 4.2% and urban unemployment 6.9%. Youth unemployment was 15.2%. The labour force participation rate was 55.5% (down 0.1 percentage points), and for women 34.6%.
